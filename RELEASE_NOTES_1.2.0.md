# Reef Bloom 1.2.0 — App Store "What's New"

Paste-ready (under 4,000 characters). Target release: October 1, 2026.

---

The big one. Reef Bloom 1.2.0 rebuilds the reef's economy around discovery — and adds a lot of fish.

MEET SKIP-7
A curator robot has set up a counter on the sand north of your reef. His Pearl Market is a walk-in shop: browse specimens that can't be hatched anywhere else, listen to his (dry) opinions on each one, and buy exactly what you see. Pearls never buy a random roll.

BUBBLES' NEST & MARKET
The Fish Nest, Fish Market and Research Surveys now share one shop screen run by Bubbles — eggs on the shelf, countdowns in the nest, expeditions on the wall, and a running commentary.

EVENTS 2.0
A new yearly calendar: Coral Bloom Festival (all April), Moonfish Migration, Pearl Tide, Shoreline Summer (six weeks), Sea Dragon Days, Golden Kelp Harvest (all October), Autumn Current, and Bioluminescence Night — two months of glowing winter. Quests earn Event Tokens; reach thresholds to unlock the Event Shop, then spend tokens on that event's exclusive species. Event species stay event-only.

BIOLUMINESCENCE NIGHT
December and January nights go deep indigo. Every glowing thing casts real light on the sand around it, blue plankton drift through the water, and now and then a bright spark floats by — tap it.

31 NEW SPECIES
Anthias, Blenny, Hawkfish, Boxfish, four butterflyfishes, Emperor Angelfish, Clown Triggerfish, Frogfish, Lionfish, Giant Trevally, Blacktip Reef Shark, Whale Shark and more — plus Leafy and Weedy Sea Dragons, a Lumen Coral that lights the deep, and the rare Yellow Chromis at the Pearl Market.

FREE ACCOUNTS
Sign in with an email and your reefs — all six slots, Classic and 3D — follow you to any device. Delete your account any time from the same screen.

AND
• Pearl Market, Nest and Events all use the new shop screens
• Home-screen greetings that know the season
• Fixes across the reef
