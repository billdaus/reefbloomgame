Both editions of Reef Bloom — the original browser game and the 3D reef on the web, iPhone and iPad — share this catalogue. The 3D edition adds 9 corals of its own (marked 3D) and stretches the level curve: a species that unlocks at Classic level n unlocks at 3D level 2n − 1, shown in the Unlock column as Lv n / 3D. Biomes unlock in the same way: the Seagrass Basin at Classic Lv 3 / 5, the Deep Twilight at Lv 6 / 11.

Rarity Tiers

Seven tiers, colour-coded the same way in the game. Rarer coral earns more Bubble Energy per tick; rarer fish lift Harmony further. Prices are the standard 🫧 Bubble Energy cost — a few species are sold for 💎 pearls at Skip-7's Pearl Market or earned with 🎟 event tokens instead.
